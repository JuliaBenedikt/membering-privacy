Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

Membering ("we", "our", or "the app") is an important dates reminder application developed by Infront. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and country preference. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from your Google account.

Date Reminders: The important dates you add (names, dates, notes, categories) are stored locally on your device using shared preferences. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

Country Selection: Your selected country is used to load public holidays relevant to your location.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide date reminders and notifications
  • To load public holidays for your selected country
  • To process your one-time payment

Payment Processing

Payments are processed securely by Verifone, a third-party payment provider. We do not store your credit card or payment details. Please refer to Verifone's Privacy Policy for their data practices.

Data Storage and Security

Account data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Your date reminders are stored locally on your device only.

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication - for account management
  • Cloud Firestore - for user profile storage
  • Verifone - for payment processing
  • Nager.Date API - for public holiday data

Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
