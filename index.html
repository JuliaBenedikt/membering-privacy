Membering ("we", "our", or "the app") is an important dates reminder application developed by Infront ehf. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and country preference. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from that account (with Apple, you may choose to hide your email, in which case we receive a private relay address).

Date Reminders: The important dates you add (names, dates, notes, categories) are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

Contacts (optional): If you choose to import birthdays, the app reads contact names and birthday dates from your device only to create reminders. Contacts are not uploaded to our servers.

Country Selection: Your selected country (and an optional second country) is used to load public and cultural holidays relevant to you.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide date and medicine reminders and notifications
  • To load public and cultural holidays for your selected countries
  • To process your one-time payment

Payment Processing

The one-time purchase that unlocks the app is processed by the platform's own in-app purchase system — Apple's App Store (In-App Purchase) on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android. We never see or store your card or payment details. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy for their data practices.

Data Storage and Security

Account data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Your date and medicine reminders are stored locally on your device only.

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication — for account management
  • Sign in with Google / Sign in with Apple — for optional sign-in
  • Cloud Firestore — for user profile storage
  • Apple In-App Purchase / Google Play Billing — for the one-time purchase
  • Public holiday data providers — for public holiday information

Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from inside the app (More → Account → Delete account), or by contacting us at infront@infront.is.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
